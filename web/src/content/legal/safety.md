**Effective date:** October 15, 2026

RenoSwap doesn't handle payment or pickups, so a little caution goes a long way.

**Before you meet**

- Keep talking in RenoSwap messages until you're ready to meet, so there's a record if something goes wrong.
- Ask for extra photos and measurements. Confirm the quantity, condition, and price before you drive over.
- **Never pay in advance** to someone you haven't met. Avoid wire transfers, gift cards, crypto, and "I'll overpay, send me the difference" offers. Those are common scams.
- Don't share your home address until you're ready to arrange pickup.

**Meeting up**

- If you can, meet in a **public, well-lit place** during the day, like a store parking lot or a police station "safe exchange" spot.
- For large items that need a home pickup, **have someone with you**. Or do a porch or driveway pickup so no one has to come inside. Tell a friend where you're going.
- Trust your gut. If something feels off, leave.

**Paying and inspecting**

- Look the item over before you pay. Check for damage, moisture, mold, and missing parts.
- Use a payment method you trust, and **only pay once you have the item**. Cash is simple. With payment apps, be sure you know who you're paying.

**Loading and handling**

- Bring the right vehicle, straps, tarps, and a helper. Many materials are heavier than they look.
- Wear gloves and closed-toe shoes. Watch for nails, sharp edges, and broken glass.
- **Don't sand, cut, or scrape** old painted or older building materials without proper precautions. They may contain lead or asbestos.
- In Texas heat, bring water and don't overdo heavy lifting.

**If something goes wrong**

- Use **Report** on the listing or user, and email RenoSwap101@gmail.com.
- If you're in danger or a crime has happened, call **911** or your local police.
